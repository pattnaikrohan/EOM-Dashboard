
This document defines the processing logic for the EOM Agent. Rules are organised into two primary sections: General Pending Invoicing and Month End Closing Checks. Each section sets out the filter criteria and business rules to be applied during end-of-month processing.




All rules are subject to periodic review. Contact your system administrator for rule amendment requests.

SECTION 1  —  GENERAL PENDING INVOICING
FILTER	DESCRIPTION
Filter 1	Business Unit
Filter 2	Branch
Filter 3	Job Type
Filter 4	Operator
Filter 5	Job Status   (Job Status ≠ CMP / IHL / CLS)

EXPORT / IMPORT RULES	EXPORT / IMPORT RULES
JOB TYPE	DATE RULE
Exports	Jobs with a Departure date within the current month
Imports	Jobs with an Arrival date within the current month
Cross-Trade	Jobs with a Departure date within the current month
Domestic	Jobs with a Departure date within the current month

SECTION 2  —  MONTH END CLOSING CHECKS	SECTION 2  —  MONTH END CLOSING CHECKS	SECTION 2  —  MONTH END CLOSING CHECKS
CHECK	DEFINITION	RULE / CONDITION
Unbilled Jobs with PROFIT	Unbilled jobs showing a profit — likely incomplete or incorrect.	Revenue = $0 AND Job Margin > $0
Unbilled Jobs with LOSS	Unbilled jobs showing a loss — likely incomplete or incorrect.	Revenue = $0 AND Job Margin < $0
Jobs with WIPs	Jobs that have outstanding Work-In-Progress amounts.	WIP > $40 AND Accrual or Cost > $40
Billed Jobs with LOSS	Jobs at CMP/IHL billing status carrying a negative profit.	Revenue > $0 AND Job Margin < $0
Billed Jobs with LOW MARGIN	Jobs at CMP/IHL billing status with a low profit margin.	Revenue > $0 AND Job Margin < 5%
Billed Jobs — EXTREME Profit	Jobs at CMP/IHL billing status with an unusually high profit margin.	Revenue > $0 AND Job Margin ≥ $5,000.00
Jobs at INV Status	Jobs currently sitting at INV (Invoiced) status.	Revenue > $0 AND Job Status = INV
Jobs at CMP — Ready to CLOSE	Jobs at CMP status that are ready to transition to CLS (Closed).	Revenue > $0, Job Profit > $0, Accrual = 0, WIP = 0
Jobs with Aged Accruals	Jobs with accrual entries older than 90 days.	Accrual Recognised Date > 90 Days
